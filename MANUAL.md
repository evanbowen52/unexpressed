# Somatic Refactor: Protocol & Field Manual

A reference manual for **post-regulation memory reconsolidation**, somatic interoception, and the **SAIC threat model**.

---

## 1. Core Operating Principles

### Acute Triage vs. Reconsolidation
This protocol is **never** used during acute dysregulation or autonomic redline. Acute physiological regulation (temperature shift, prolonged exhale, somatic grounding, physical movement) happens offline in the physical environment.

The protocol begins only after physiological baseline calm has been restored. Its objective is not symptom management, but **memory reconsolidation**: juxtaposing the old conditioned somatic alarm against present-day physical reality to dismantle automated neural threat loops.

### Interoception Over Narrative Labels
Cognitive narrative labels ("frustrated," "anxious," "offended") trigger left-brain storytelling and conceptual rumination. This protocol bypasses emotion words in favor of direct interoceptive coordinates:
* **Raw sensation** (`Tight`, `Hot`, `Heavy`, `Hollow`, `Buzzy`, `Clenched`)
* **Anatomical locus** (`Chest`, `Throat`, `Jaw`, `Gut`, `Shoulders`, `Head`)
* **Core threat driver** (The SAIC Model)

### Frictionless Telemetry
Tracking state shifts must not impose cognitive load. The workflow maps state transitions using coarse groupings and discrete telemetry:
`code1 --> duration --> code2:loops --> duration --> code3`

## 2. The SAIC Core Threat Model

All subcortical alarm responses in this protocol trace back to one or more of four primitive existential threats (**SAIC**):

### 1. Safety
* **Domain:** Biological survival & somatic integrity.
* **Internal Assumption:** *"I will be physically harmed, starved, depleted, or overwhelmed."*
* **Triggers:** Physical vulnerability, illness, scene residue, medical decline, somatic pain, resource depletion.

### 2. Agency
* **Domain:** Operant capacity, freedom of movement, and boundary enforcement.
* **Internal Assumption:** *"I am trapped, forced, powerless, or my boundaries are erased."*
* **Triggers:** Caregiver burden, bureaucratic deadlock, system friction, coercion, task paralysis, inability to alter biological or external realities.

### 3. Identity
* **Domain:** Coherence of self-model, competence, and moral integrity.
* **Internal Assumption:** *"I am flawed, exposed, inadequate, or failing my own standards."*
* **Triggers:** Mistakes, criticism, moral distress ("calm = complicity"), perceived incompetence, meta-judgment at one's own emotional reactions ("Third Arrow").

### 4. Connection
* **Domain:** Relational attachment, social safety, and belonging.
* **Internal Assumption:** *"I am unloved, misunderstood, cast out, or relationally severed."*
* **Triggers:** Tone shifts, dismissal, silence/unanswered messages, familial abandonment, grief, interpersonal conflict.

## 3. The 4-Quadrant Somatic Coordinate System

State telemetry maps onto a 2-axis coordinate system: **Valence** (Negative to Positive) and **Arousal** (Low to High).

```
                      HIGH AROUSAL
                           ▲
             RED           │          YELLOW
       (Alarm / Threat)    │    (Focus / Engagement)
                           │
  NEGATIVE ────────────────┼──────────────── POSITIVE
  VALENCE                  │                 VALENCE
             BLUE          │          GREEN
      (Depleted / Freeze)  │     (Settled / Ease)
                           │
                           ▼
                      LOW AROUSAL
```

### Quadrant R — High Arousal, Negative Valence (Alarm / Bracing)
* **R1:** Background irritation $(-1, 6)$
* **R2:** Noticeable alarm $(-2, 7)$
* **R3:** Significant bracing $(-3, 8)$
* **R4:** High threat spike $(-4, 9)$
* **R5:** Full SAIC Redline $(-5, 10)$ *(Charge $> 5$ or R4/R5 flags state as too hot for reconsolidation)*

### Quadrant Y — High Arousal, Positive Valence (Mobilized / Drive)
* **Y1:** Focus / Interest $(+1, 6)$
* **Y2:** Engaged energy $(+2, 7)$
* **Y3:** Drive / Momentum $(+3, 8)$
* **Y4:** High activation $(+4, 9)$
* **Y5:** Peak excitement $(+5, 10)$

### Quadrant B — Low Arousal, Negative Valence (Flat / Depleted / Freeze)
* **B1:** Quiet / Disengaged $(-1, 4)$
* **B2:** Low / Flat $(-2, 3)$
* **B3:** Heavy fatigue $(-3, 2)$
* **B4:** Depleted $(-4, 1)$
* **B5:** Shutdown / Collapse $(-5, 0)$

### Quadrant G — Low Arousal, Positive Valence (Settled / Regulated)
* **G1:** Natural Ease $(+1, 4)$
* **G2:** Settled $(+2, 3)$
* **G3:** Grounded / Calm $(+3, 2)$
* **G4:** Deep rest $(+4, 1)$
* **G5:** Deep Absorption $(+5, 0)$

## 4. Protocol Workflow Execution (Steps 0–6)

### Step 0: Passive Gating
* **Inquiry:** *"Are you calm enough?"*
* **Rule:** If physical charge is $> 5/10$ or breathing is shallow/rapid, stop. Regulate offline first. The app is never used for acute dysregulation triage.

### Step 1: Bare Facts
* **Domain:** `Interpersonal` | `Environmental` | `Personal`
* **Pattern:** `Tone / Dismissal` | `Criticism / Correction` | `Boundary / Demand` | `Silence / Ambiguity` | `Plan / System Friction` | `Sensory / Scene` | `+ Fast Reflex`
* **Modifiers:** `Memory Loop` | `Third Arrow`
* Optional single-sentence factual note (strictly descriptive, no narrative editorializing).

### Step 2: Body Snapshot (`code1`)
* Tap starting quadrant coordinate (`R1–R5`, `Y1–Y5`, `B1–B5`, `G1–G5`).
* Select somatic sensations (`Tight`, `Hot`, `Heavy`, `Hollow`, `Buzzy`, `Clenched`).
* Select anatomical locations (`Chest`, `Throat`, `Jaw`, `Gut`, `Shoulders`, `Head`).
* Select Charge level ($1$–$10$). Ratings $> 5$ or coordinates `R4`/`R5` prompt offline regulation.

### Step 3: Identify System Assumption (SAIC)
* Multi-select active primitive threat vectors (`Safety`, `Agency`, `Identity`, `Connection`).
* Subcortical assumption prompts surface dynamically to bring implicit predictions into conscious awareness.

### Step 4: Reality Juxtaposition & Softening (`code2:xn`)
* Multi-select present reality anchor chips (`Safe room`, `Event is over`, `Adult now`, `Calm ≠ Complicity`, `Old wiring ≠ Failure`, `I have options`).
* Execute somatic softening interventions (`Drop shoulders`, `Soft belly`, `Unclench jaw`, `Long exhale`, `Feel heels`).
* Track loop recurrence (`Once`, `Few`, `Many`).
* Tap intermediate quadrant coordinate (`code2`).

### Step 5: Re-Check (`code3`) & Autonomic Signals
* Tap final state coordinate (`code3`).
* Check for autonomic parasympathetic transition signals:
  * `Sigh` (spontaneous deep exhalation)
  * `Yawn` (cranial nerve reset)
  * `Warmth` (peripheral vasodilation)
  * `Gut gurgle` (enteric nervous system reactivation / peristalsis)
  * `Quiet settle` (somatic still point)

### Step 6: Telemetry & Obsidian Export
* Compiles telemetry string: `code1 --> duration --> code2:loops --> duration --> code3`
* Formats structured Markdown with YAML frontmatter directly to system clipboard.
* Retains persistent record in local storage (`localStorage`).

## 4. Trigger Mapping Field Guide

This field guide provides baseline patterns, SAIC threat diagnoses, and present-reality anchors for common high-friction scenarios.

### Category 1: Caregiving & Family

#### Parent with Dementia (Repetition, Confusion, Resistance to Care)
* **Step 1 Source:** `Interpersonal` + `Plan / System Friction` (or `Boundary / Demand`)
* **SAIC Driver:** `Agency` (helpless to stop neurological decline) + `Connection` (grief and loss of the parent you knew)
* **Reality Anchor:** `Adult now`, `Event is over` *(Note: Disease biology is driving the behavior, not willful defiance)*

#### Parent Health Crisis / ER / Medical Decline
* **Step 1 Source:** `Interpersonal` + `Plan / System Friction` (`+ Fast Reflex` if emergency call)
* **SAIC Driver:** `Safety` (mortality, physical frailty) + `Agency` (inability to control biology)
* **Reality Anchor:** `Safe room`, `I have options`

#### Siblings Checked Out / Unequal Load / Second-Guessing
* **Step 1 Source:** `Interpersonal` + `Boundary / Demand` (or `Silence / Ambiguity`)
* **SAIC Driver:** `Agency` (trapped carrying burden alone) + `Identity` (unfairly judged) + `Connection`
* **Reality Anchor:** `I have options`, `Adult now` *(Note: Their bandwidth or denial is not your moral failing)*

#### Replaying a Tense Family Call or Care Decision Later
* **Step 1 Source:** `Interpersonal` + `Criticism / Correction` + `[Memory Loop]`
* **SAIC Driver:** `Identity` + `Agency`
* **Reality Anchor:** `Safe room`, `Event is over`

### Category 3: Interpersonal & Everyday Friction

#### Kids Facing Physical or Mental Health Challenges

* **SAIC Driver:** `Connection` (fear of disconnection/abandonment) + `Agency` (inability to prepare)
* **Reality Anchor:** `Safe room`, `I have options`
